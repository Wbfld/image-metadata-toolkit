Why product teams choose it

  • Privacy findings are semantic and hide sensitive values by default.
  • Strict policies produce no output when inspection or mutation coverage is insufficient.
  • Duplicates, conflicts, unknown structures, provenance, and completeness remain visible.
  • Supported edits are atomic, reparsed, and checked against encoded image-payload hashes.
  • Network access and official C2PA verification live behind separate explicit entry points.

Trust boundaries

  • Metadata values are rendered as text, never as HTML.
  • Raw sensitive values are hidden by default in privacy output.
  • Unknown and opaque structures remain visible as fail-closed risks.
  • C2PA inventory is structural and never implies signature verification.
  • Preservation evidence compares encoded payloads and does not claim pixel equivalence.
